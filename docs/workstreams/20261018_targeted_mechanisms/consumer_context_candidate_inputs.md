# 消费者处境与政策背景：候选输入

状态：2026-10-18离线草案，尚未进入Simulation。下文是准备插入原提示的公开背景块，不替换本人真实先验、不增加任何本期SCE答案，不把其他调查的人群特征冒充本人特征。每块来源和日期见`consumer_context_evidence.json`。

## 2020-09

Public consumer-survey background, published August 28, 2020:

The University of Michigan reported weak current economic sentiment alongside expectations of recovery from the shutdown. Income and job uncertainty remained important, while pandemic demand had raised some prices faced by middle- and lower-income households. This describes another survey's respondents, not your own recorded circumstances.

## 2022-06

Public consumer-survey background, preliminary release published June 10, 2022:

The University of Michigan reported strong concerns about gasoline, shortages, and purchasing power. Consumers could expect wage gains yet worry that prices would rise faster; weaker economic prospects and concerns about continuing inflation appeared together. These are reported consumer views, not instructions about how you should answer.

## 2025-03

Public consumer-survey background, published February 21, 2025:

The University of Michigan reported concerns that tariffs would raise future prices. Some consumers considered earlier durable purchases to avoid anticipated price increases, while the economic outlook weakened. These concerns preceded a corresponding realized inflation surge and are not a guarantee that prices will rise.

Public trade-policy statements:

- March 3, 2025: USTR announced the administration's trade agenda, including rebalancing trade and supporting domestic production. These are stated policy goals, not measured outcomes.
- March 12, 2025: USTR responded to announced EU retaliatory tariffs. This is the US agency's statement about a trade dispute, not an independent estimate of household inflation.

## 设计约束

第一轮只比较上述新增公开背景，不给另一调查的通胀预期数值，不附“你应该更悲观/更关注关税”等行为命令。原有新闻不删除，不合并不同日期的后续发展。政策与消费者叙述打包加入，只能识别这一信息组合的增量，不能据此分别识别关税、情绪或购物行为的因果效应。

同一期公众信号不自动产生个体差异。若只改善群体中心、仍不改善个人分布，就保留宏观收益并如实说明微观机制未恢复。跨人身份、行业/购物责任、党派倾向均不由这些报告倒推。
